## Precisely scoped files

Separate treatment applies exclusively to these three graphics files:

- `Brand/Wupsy-Logo-v2.png` – source of the Wupsy app icon.
- `Brand/Wupsy-MenuBar-Icon-v1.png` – Wupsy menu-bar graphic.
- `Brand/WSS_Logo.png` – WSS graphic in the DMG background.

This policy does not additionally release them under MIT as freely reusable graphics resources. No other graphics, synthetic test images, masks, vector fields or user wallpapers are classified as WSS branding by default.

The scope also covers only the technically generated representations of these three graphics:

- `Wupsy.iconset/icon_16x16.png`, `icon_16x16@2x.png`, `icon_32x32.png`, `icon_32x32@2x.png`, `icon_128x128.png`, `icon_128x128@2x.png`, `icon_256x256.png`, `icon_256x256@2x.png`, `icon_512x512.png` and `icon_512x512@2x.png`, generated from the app logo; the actual working location is temporary.
- The resulting `Wupsy.app/Contents/Resources/Wupsy.icns` and its unchanged copy as `.VolumeIcon.icns` on the DMG.
- The unchanged copy `Wupsy.app/Contents/Resources/Wupsy-MenuBar-Icon-v1.png` and its display as a menu-bar template image.
- The representation of `Brand/WSS_Logo.png` in the generated DMG image `.background/background.png`, and display of these graphics in the app, Finder or project documentation.

The same mappings apply when output directories differ. The scope covers the respective graphic content, not technical containers, other image content, documentation or scripts. Technical generation or copying does not grant an additional MIT license to separately treated graphic content. The project's own build scripts and configurations remain MIT-licensed.

The unchanged graphics files may also be redistributed as part of an unchanged copy of the official source repository and kept locally to view and edit the MIT-licensed components. This permission does not make them freely reusable graphics resources or permit incorporating them into other projects.

Required MIT copyright, permission and third-party notices must be retained when redistributing. Permission to redistribute the unchanged graphics does not replace these obligations.

## Modified builds and forks

For your own modified builds, forks or derivative versions, the official WSS graphics listed above must be removed or replaced with your own graphics unless separate express permission has been obtained. This also applies to generated app/volume icons, menu-bar representations and the graphic content in the DMG background. Technical asset names may be retained when replacing graphics to avoid unnecessary code changes.

Your versions must not use this branding to suggest that they are official WSS releases. Further use or modification of the separately treated graphics requires separate permission to the extent corresponding rights exist and have not already been granted otherwise.
